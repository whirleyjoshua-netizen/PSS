# Owner answers: cellular shades guide

Josh (owner), 2026-11-06, answering the "Owner questions" in research.md. These are the owner's own statements about how PSS sells and recommends cellular shades, so they count as a valid source for PSS claims, cited as "owner answer #n". Where an answer allows something new, the fact-checker may add it to the copy. Where it rules something out, the fact-checker must remove it.

1. **Cell construction.** We recommend **double cell**, with **1 1/2" cells rather than 3/4"** cells.
2. **Cordless.** Cordless is an option on every cellular shade we sell.
3. **AERC label.** Josh asked us to check. Findings, from neutral sources:
   - The U.S. Department of Energy reported that Hunter Douglas received the first energy performance rating from the Attachments Energy Rating Council (AERC) for its cellular shades: https://www.energy.gov/node/4442808
   - AERC-certified products carry a Warm Climate and a Cool Climate rating. Anyone can look a product up with the AERC Certified Product Search at https://www.aercenergyrating.org.
   - Nothing found shows whether the Alta or Norman cellular lines are AERC rated, so don't claim it for them.
   - The copy may say that some of the cellular shades we carry have an AERC energy rating, and that the label has a Warm Climate rating, which is the one that matters in Las Vegas. Verify the energy.gov source before using it. A Hunter Douglas product name needs ® plus a descriptor, e.g. "Duette® Honeycomb Shades". Don't use HD marketing copy.
4. **Day sleepers.** Josh: cellular shades are the better choice for sleeping all day: they block heat, and they leave smaller light gaps at the sides. **PENDING:** this contradicts the published pages, which recommend a blackout roller with side channels (the day-sleepers guide, the FAQ, the west-facing and roman guides). Josh has been asked whether to change those pages too. Until he rules, don't change the day-sleeper advice in this guide.
5. **Neighborhoods.** Yes: the guide may name MacDonald Highlands and other Henderson neighborhoods.
6. **Light or reflective backing facing the glass.** No. Don't recommend it.
7. **Installs.** Installing cellular shades is straightforward and doesn't need explaining. Leave out any install walkthrough.
